# Props Edge / Parlay history

Open **Props Edge → Parlay history** and choose:

- **Published model tickets**: all unique combinations published from this release
  onward are saved before their first game. The scheduled keyless Props refresh
  grades them from the existing final-stat leg results. Expand a ticket to see its
  original selections, line, illustrative price, final statistics and result.
- **My actual wagers**: use **Log actual bet** in Parlay lab, enter your sportsbook's
  actual combined American price, stake and book, and save. The entire structured
  ticket is retained in the existing private ledger, including across shared-ledger
  sync. Outcomes update on opening/refreshing Props Edge, not while the browser is
  closed. The public server never receives your personal wagers.

Filter by result, first-game Toronto date, or player/matchup/book. Full-ticket JSON
export preserves details, not just a summary. The existing shared-ledger backup
also retains the native parlay snapshot. Public model history is committed with
model data and does not disappear when the current slate changes. Private wagers
remain subject to browser storage and the user's configured sync; keep a backup.

## Settlement boundaries

A model ticket wins only when all its legs win; any ordinary losing leg fails
the model combination. Pushes, partial voids or conflicting results require review;
all-void model tickets are listed separately. These are research outcomes, **not
profitable bets** at estimated or multiplied single-leg prices. Multiple tickets
can overlap and are not independent statistical samples.

Actual tickets settle automatically only after **all** legs have verified final
Win/Loss results. A missing stat is not a zero. Exact event, player, team, market,
side and line must match. Any push or void is left Pending with a Book review
label; SGP cancellation/repricing rules differ and reduced prices are not guessed.
Confirm the sportsbook's settlement manually in My ledger, updating its combined
price if required. Manual result edits are not overwritten by automatic grading.

Existing text-only personal parlays remain visible but cannot be safely rebuilt
into structured selections; their results remain manual. Public historical tickets
are not invented from old leg outcomes or today's model. The new archive starts
prospectively. Existing betting records and forecasts are not deleted or rewritten.
