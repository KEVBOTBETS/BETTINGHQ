How to read these predictions

These are expected stat averages if the player continues in their recent role. Public depth charts identify expected starters; meaningful recent usage identifies regular contributors. An injury replacement is a candidate, not a confirmed starter. Questionable starters retain their uncertain status. They are research estimates, not sportsbook lines or a playing-time guarantee. Future weeks are early outlooks using today’s form.

— means unavailable or not applicable, not zero. A decimal touchdown projection such as 0.7 is an average count, not a 70% chance to score. Passing TD and TD scored are separate statistics.

Confirmed unavailable players are excluded. Expected starters on bye teams have no stat line. Offensive linemen and long snappers remain in the roster list even when no individual box-score statistic is modelled. The sample column shows the maximum games used; individual stats may use fewer games.

Once a game starts, the last saved pregame projection is retained. Weeks before this feature’s first capture have no forecast history; historical predictions are never invented.

Sources: ESPN public depth charts, rosters and completed regular-season box scores. Weekly form uses the last six weeks of the current season. Two or three games are a small sample; relief-only QB passing is withheld. No API key is required.
