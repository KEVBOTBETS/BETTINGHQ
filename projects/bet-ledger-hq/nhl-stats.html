How to read these predictions

These are conditional expected counts, not sportsbook lines or calibrated probabilities. Confirm participation and the exact offered price before using a betting market.

— means unsupported or unavailable. Real measured zeroes display as 0.0. Starter and rotation labels are expected roles, not confirmed active lineups. Questionable players retain an availability warning. Small samples remain uncertain; injury replacements do not automatically inherit another player’s workload.

NBA: current-team per-minute rates from up to eight completed games within six weeks, multiplied by recent expected minutes. NHL: current-season official counts with recent form where reported; goals use the existing shrunk goal model. Goalie numbers depend on playing time. Historical save percentage is descriptive.

Future dates use today’s roster and form. Saved pregame forecasts freeze after kickoff; historical forecasts begin with the first capture.
