READ THE RECORD

Accuracy and returns have different samples.

Cover accuracy is wins divided by wins plus losses. Pushes, unplayed games, missing lines and exact no-edge calls stay outside that percentage. Margin error includes verified scores even when a market line was unavailable.

Archived pregame lines can support historical cover grading. Their quote freshness and offered price were not saved, so no price is assumed and no return is calculated. New prices must be observed within 12 hours and saved before the game starts. The first forecast, first line and first qualified pick stay frozen.

Qualified picks start prospectively with this release: positive adjusted edge, LEAN/GOOD/BEST, not held, and a verified fresh price. Their selection can differ from the whole-sheet call. Returns illustrate one unit per priced selection, including pushes; they are not your actual wager profit. No profitable-sport claim is made from accuracy alone.

NHL/MLB forecasts without a recorded puck/run line remain visible as unscored margin predictions. NBA/NCAAB do not yet have qualified models.
