Sources, limits and how models earn promotion

Current forecasts are the baseline. Market probabilities require both opposite prices at the same line and book. Ensemble weights require 40 settled, paired prospective games; they remain experimental. Model review requires at least 100 paired games over 28 dates and a date-clustered comparison. A favorable comparison is a review signal, never an automatic release.

Prop simulations are conditional on participation. Their workload estimates do not verify a starting role. Core and Longshot describe ticket size; neither is a confidence guarantee. A combined sportsbook price is required before ticket expected value can be calculated.
