KNOW WHAT YOU'RE USING

Behind the model

How the forecast works

Team scoring and goals allowed feed a Poisson goal model, with a modest home-ice adjustment and back-to-back rest adjustment. Prior-season rates regress toward 3.05 goals and blend out over 20 current-season games. Regulation ties split equally between the teams, with one deciding goal for OT/SO.

These are uncalibrated model estimates. Team goal-rate projections are not shot-based expected goals (xG). Goalie identities and injuries are context; individual talent is not yet a model input.

What makes a qualified pick?

A regular-season or playoff game within 24 hours, both starting goalies confirmed, complete same-book two-sided prices observed within 90 minutes, and at least 3% model expected return. For priced picks, the baseline probability is blended 25% model / 75% same-book no-vig market. Disagreements over 15 percentage points or returns over 15% are held for review. One qualified market per game goes to the daily ticket.

The schedule refreshes through the existing GitHub workflow. Manual research remains available when a feed cannot supply prices or starting goalies. No personal API keys or paid data subscription are required.
