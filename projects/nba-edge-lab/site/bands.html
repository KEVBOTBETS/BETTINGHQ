How to read and use this lab

Example: a team can have a 70% chance to win but only a 20% chance to win by 6–10 points. Three 20% bands together have about a 0.8% chance under the independence assumption.

Match the exact ranges, named team and overtime rules at your sportsbook. The preset formats are templates, not a claim that a book currently offers them. Prices and payouts are not inferred from forecasts. Check actual prices before placing anything.

The existing NBA scoring baseline supplies the signed average margin and a normal margin spread (currently 12 points). Band probabilities use integer boundaries and the same baseline win probability. No new injury adjustment or statistical validation is implied. Ticket estimates multiply distinct-game probabilities, assume independence and do not account for shared model error. Tickets overlap; they are alternatives, not independent chances to win.

Forecasts and source observations must be no more than four hours old. Started, canceled, postponed and stale games are excluded. No selections are added just to fill a ticket.
